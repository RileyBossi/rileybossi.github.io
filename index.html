Lessons Learned

  • Security is about fundamentals, not magic tools. Most breaches stem from weak credentials, excessive privileges, and poor hygiene.
  • Logs tell a story — context makes them actionable. Correlating PowerShell, authentication, and DNS anomalies uncovers real threats hidden in noise.
  • Threat actor behavior matters more than malware signatures. Understanding TTPs improves detection and incident response capabilities.
  • Staying informed is a discipline, not a task. Continuous learning is part of security hygiene.
