Lessons Learned

  • A security alert is only a starting point and must be validated using supporting evidence.
  • Effective investigations require correlating endpoint, identity, email, and network activity.
  • Establishing a timeline makes it easier to understand how an incident began and what systems were affected.
  • Indicators of compromise should be evaluated in context rather than treated as proof of malicious activity by themselves.
  • MITRE ATT&CK helps translate technical findings into recognizable attacker tactics and techniques.
  • Clear documentation is essential when escalating an alert or handing an investigation to another analyst.
